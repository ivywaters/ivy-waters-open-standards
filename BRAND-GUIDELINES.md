# Brand use and official status

The Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license applies to the
Standards content and documentation in this repository. It does not grant
trademark rights.

**Ivy Waters International**, **Ivy Waters International Standards**,
**IvyTracks**, the Ivy Waters crest, and related trade dress identify Ivy
Waters International and its official products and publications.

You may make truthful referential use of the framework name to identify and
attribute the source. You may not use Ivy Waters names, marks, crest, or trade
dress in a way that suggests an adaptation, product, service, organization, or
publication is sponsored, affiliated, endorsed, certified, or officially
issued by Ivy Waters International when it is not.

The repository includes one official Ivy Waters International publisher
lockup so readers can identify the original release. That image remains
protected brand artwork, is not licensed under CC BY 4.0, and may not be used
on an adaptation in a way that implies endorsement. Other crest and brand
artwork is intentionally excluded.

## Official Standards lockup

`assets/ivy-waters-international-standards-lockup.png` identifies material
published by Ivy Waters International as part of the official Standards
release. It contains the Ivy Waters crest. It may be preserved when sharing an

status, sponsorship, certification, affiliation, or endorsement.

## Reference badge

`assets/ivy-waters-open-standards-reference-badge.png` deliberately contains
no crest. Ivy Waters International permits the unmodified badge to be copied
and displayed solely to make the truthful statement that a work references or
maps to one or more public Standards identifiers, provided that:

- the badge appears with the Standards release version and a link to
  `https://ivywaters.com/standards`;
- the surrounding presentation clearly identifies the work's actual publisher;
- the work does not claim or imply certification, compliance, accreditation,
  compatibility approval, affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement by Ivy
  Waters International; and
- the badge is not altered, combined with another logo, or made more prominent
  than the publisher's own identity.

This limited reference-badge permission is separate from CC BY 4.0. It does
not grant permission to use the Ivy Waters crest, official Standards lockup,
trade dress, or any other Ivy Waters mark.
