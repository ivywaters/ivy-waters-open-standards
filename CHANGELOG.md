# Changelog

The Ivy Waters International Standards use the versioning rules in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Newest entries appear first.

## [2.2.0] - 2026-09-03

MINOR: publish the public-safe catalog under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International. Owner approval: Faiz, 2026-09-03. No standard identifier,
capability, guide, domain, or alignment meaning changed.

Catalog truth: 138 standards across 52 guides and 10 stages.

### Changed

- Permit copying, redistribution, adaptation, and commercial use with
  attribution, a license link, and an indication of changes.
- Keep Ivy Waters names, crests, logos, and marks outside the content license;
  adaptations may not imply endorsement.
- Establish a screened standalone public repository and retain the private
  registrar translation overlay outside every public artifact.
- Advance release identity to v2.2.0 because the public reuse contract changed
  even though catalog content did not.

## Earlier history

The complete pre-public-repository changelog remains in the canonical Ivy
Waters ecosystem repository and should be imported before the first external
release. It records v2.1.1 and the 2.1.0 public numbering baseline, plus the
earlier 0.x catalog-development history without renumbering those entries.
