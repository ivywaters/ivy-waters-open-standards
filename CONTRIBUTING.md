# Contributing

Thank you for helping make the Standards clearer, more useful, and easier to
implement.

## Good contributions

- report a typo, ambiguity, inaccessible phrase, or JSON inconsistency;
- show a real implementation need using a permanent standard identifier;
- propose clearer examples without narrowing the routes through which a
  learner can demonstrate a capability;
- improve citation, accessibility, validation, or developer documentation; or
- identify a public-boundary, attribution, privacy, or external-framework
  concern.

## Catalog changes

An official catalog change must follow the Standards governance process:

1. state the exact identifier and current wording;
2. provide the exact proposed wording and rationale;
3. explain whether capability meaning changes;
4. preserve the identifier when meaning is unchanged;
5. use a new identifier when capability meaning changes;
6. receive dated Ivy Waters owner review; and
7. update the version and changelog with regenerated artifacts.

Opening a pull request does not make a proposal an official Ivy Waters
standard. Maintainers may discuss, revise, or decline proposals to preserve the
framework's coherence and permanent-ID contract.

## Independent adaptations

You do not need to contribute an adaptation upstream. CC BY 4.0 permits you to
publish one independently. Attribute the source, link the license, describe
your changes, retain earlier modification notices, and avoid implying Ivy
Waters endorsement or official status.

## Privacy and third-party material

Never include learner names, educational records, family data, private
communications, or identifying images in an issue or pull request. Do not
submit text, mappings, logos, or images copied from another curriculum or
framework unless you can establish a compatible right and license to do so.
