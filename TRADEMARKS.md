# Trademarks and official status

Standards content and documentation in this repository. It does not grant
trademark rights.

**Ivy Waters International**, **The Ivy Waters International Standards**,
**IvyTracks**, the Ivy Waters crest, and related trade dress identify Ivy
Waters International and its official products and publications.

You may make truthful referential use of the framework name to identify and
attribute the source. You may not use Ivy Waters names, marks, crest, or trade
dress in a way that suggests an adaptation, product, service, organization, or
publication is sponsored, affiliated, endorsed, certified, or officially
issued by Ivy Waters International when it is not.

The repository includes one official Ivy Waters International publisher
lockup so readers can identify the original release. That image remains
protected brand artwork, is not licensed under CC BY 4.0, and may not be used
on an adaptation in a way that implies endorsement. Other crest and brand
artwork is intentionally excluded.
