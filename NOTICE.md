# Attribution and modification notices

## Whole framework

Use this notice when reproducing or integrating the unmodified catalog:

> *Ivy Waters International Standards* v2.2.0, © 2026 Ivy Waters
> International, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
> https://ivywaters.com/standards

## Individual standard

Include the permanent identifier and title:

> Ivy Waters International. “{Title}” ({ID}), in *Ivy Waters
> International Standards* v2.2.0 (2026), licensed under CC BY 4.0.
> https://ivywaters.com/standards/{ID}

## Adaptation

CC BY 4.0 requires an indication when licensed material has been modified.
Use language that makes the source, your changes, and the lack of endorsement
easy to understand:

> Adapted from *Ivy Waters International Standards* v2.2.0, © 2026 Ivy
> Waters International, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Changes made: {briefly
> describe changes}. This adaptation is independent and is not endorsed by
> Ivy Waters International.

If you receive adapted material carrying an earlier modification notice,
retain an indication of those earlier modifications as required by CC BY 4.0.

## Machine-readable attribution

The release JSON provides:

- the title and publisher;
- the version and publication date;
- the copyright and CC BY 4.0 notice;
- the SPDX identifier and canonical license URL;
- the preferred attribution string;
- the canonical public source; and
- a recommended modification notice.

Reasonable attribution may be adapted to the medium and context. Do not state
or imply that Ivy Waters International endorses a use, adaptation, product, or
organization.
